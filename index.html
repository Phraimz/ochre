PROJECT 001

The
Ochre

The Ochre is an architectural visualization project developed to explore how material, proportion, light and spatial relationships can work together to create a distinct atmosphere.

Rather than experiencing the project through static drawings alone, the space is presented as an immersive virtual environment.

Visitors can move through the design and experience the architecture from different viewpoints before construction begins.

01 — THE CONCEPT

Architecture
beyond drawings.

The Ochre explores how architecture can be communicated through atmosphere, material and movement rather than drawings alone.

Warm tones, controlled lighting and carefully considered proportions create a residential environment that feels both contemporary and grounded.
